Installing on macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag TimeBunny into Applications.
  2. Launch it. On first run macOS asks for Screen Recording, Accessibility and Input Monitoring — TimeBunny needs all three to record activity while a shift is running.
  3. Grant them in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen TimeBunny so the permissions take effect.
  4. Sign in with your Bunny Agency account.

Installing on Windows

  1. Run the .exe. SmartScreen will warn that the publisher is unknown — that is expected until the installer is code-signed. Choose More info → Run anyway.
  2. The installer sets TimeBunny up for the current user; no administrator rights are needed.
  3. Sign in with your Bunny Agency account.

Verifying your download

Every installer's SHA-256 is printed on its card above. To check what you downloaded:

# macOS
shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/TimeBunny_*.dmg

# Windows (PowerShell)
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 $HOME\Downloads\TimeBunny_*-setup.exe

On macOS you can also confirm Apple's notarisation directly:

spctl -a -vvv /Applications/TimeBunny.app
# expect: accepted / source=Notarized Developer ID

What TimeBunny records

It tracks only while you have a shift running, and what it records is immutable — you cannot edit, blur or delete your own tracked data, and neither can anyone else without leaving an audit trail. See the privacy summary for the details.
